## Design Context

### Users
Buyers and sellers of secondhand goods in Ho Chi Minh City, browsing on phones and desktops in Vietnamese or English.

### Brand Personality
Simple, welcoming, practical. Help people find useful things and sell what they no longer need.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user requested an appealing minimalist online marketplace focused directly on selling and browsing. Use warm neutral surfaces, one quiet green accent, clear sans-serif typography, and product-led layouts. Preserve light and dark modes.

### Design Principles
- Put search, categories, real listings, and selling ahead of storytelling.
- Keep navigation short and actions explicit.
- Use compact, consistent layouts across marketplace and supporting pages.
- Preserve bilingual labels, accessible controls, and honest loading, empty, and error states.
- Prefer existing assets and lightweight components; no decorative video or scroll choreography.
