# Marketplace surface

Scope: the whole Vòng web app (home, browse, listing, sell, payment and key, your listings, messages, saved, about, FAQ, contact, admin). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: anyone in Ho Chi Minh City finding secondhand things nearby, or selling what they no longer use, mostly on a phone, in Vietnamese or English.

Owner's constraints: lighter colors, better fonts, no AI look or AI-sounding copy. Avoid: too playful, too plain, too many boxes. Keep the name Vòng, the V loop mark, and both languages. Light theme only. No sample listings or generated images.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Vòng is a well-kept pass-đồ group thread. Every listing is a post from a named person in a district, and every next step is a reply. It refuses the category-default photo grid with an orange accent and the wall of boxed cards.

OWN-WORLD: White page, pale sky thread (#EEF5FF) behind the feed, quiet received-bubble grey (#F1F3F6), ink #15202B, one action blue #1463F3, coral #FF5A4E only for unread. Be Vietnam Pro for everything; prices tabular 600. Posts are speech bubbles with one tight corner toward the author's avatar; hairlines and space group everything else. No uppercase labels, no decorative shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees what is for sale nearby as posts, narrows by district or category like switching threads, opens a post, and replies to the seller. A seller posts by writing, pays the flat fee, and goes live with the emailed key.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the V mark, a composer-shaped search field and "Đăng bán / Sell". District names sit under it as thread tabs. The feed follows: avatar, name, district, time, then the post bubble with photo, title, price and condition, and a "Reply to seller" line beneath. Desktop keeps the feed at reading width with a quiet right column for the fee and categories.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: "Reply to seller" opens the private chat with the post quoted at the top; new messages slide up into place in 180 ms.

FORM: Pass-đồ group chat, 4th on the ordered list, seed key 69bfbecb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
